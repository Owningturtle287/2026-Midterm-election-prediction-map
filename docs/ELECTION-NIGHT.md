## Method and limits

Original 3:22 p.m. forecast is immutable. Called races follow DDHQ. Uncalled expectations combine original priors, reported margins, county/town vote estimates, 2024 geographic comparisons and DDHQ live Pulse where available. The county check extrapolates current local candidate shares into estimated remaining votes, imputing nonreporting places from 2024 presidential shares plus the observed local swing. It is a sensitivity aid, not a fitted or calibrated vote model; ballot mode and turnout estimates can change. No post-election poll-quality adjustment is inferred from these returns.

Judgmental plausible ranges: House 250–270 Democrats; Senate 52–56 Democratic-aligned; governors 29–33 Democrats. These are not calibrated confidence intervals. A favorite map is not the same as an expected value across uncertain outcomes.

The House change is net +25 Democratic seats relative to the original map: 26 Republican picks now favor Democrats (including the three called misses), while MI-07 moves to Barrett. Very narrow races can easily shift this total.
